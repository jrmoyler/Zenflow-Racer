# Actual exported mesh review

Reviewed the exported GLBs under the fixed studio camera in front, side and rear views.
This checks three-dimensional continuity, identifiable components, palette hierarchy and
wearable equipment. It is not a pixel match to a supplied Wave 2 hero sheet; those individual
hero images were not present. Legacy exact-fidelity acceptance remains unestablished.

| Asset | Observed identity and continuity |
|---|---|
| Ledger | Violet hex grille and side honeycomb, short crystal bonnet and low faceted tail. Initial grille lacked an enclosure; the final XY-extruded carbon fascia now gives the cells a dark recessed seat and a pearl boundary. |
| Terra | Heavy blue beam chassis and white overpass arches frame rubber wheels; broad blunt nose and block lights read differently from the lighter tourers. |
| Obsidian | Angular split canards and raised rear blades make a lower, longer silhouette; carbon shoulders and orange slit signatures remain visible from side and rear. |

| Gaia | Leaf-shaped fenders and paired seed pods alter both front and rear silhouette. Green vine ribbons surround the body; the single blue trace stays thin and subordinate. |
| Nomad | Raised arches, knobby rubber tyre blocks, latched side cases and three bronze luggage rails distinguish the hauler from white-wheel road karts. |
| Eon | Smooth enclosed nose/rear cowls, linked aqua rings and a center spine light make a restrained medical tourer. |

The charcoal uniform, raised collar, shoulder pads, harness, helmets, mirrored visors, gloves
and boots remain complete on all eight. Hands meet the steering rim; the seat supports the
hips; wheel pivots and visible suspension remain aligned across views. Metal and pearl
surfaces reflect the studio lights while the suit remains matte. Small lamp/ribbon emissions
appear lighter than the stored accent HEX values under the studio exposure.

The initial Ledger views and first backing attempt are retained in `ledger-initial` and
`ledger-backing-initial`. The final correction uses the proper XY shape adapter because the
shared sculptedPanel function is designed for XZ coachwork surfaces. This is a documented
changed method, not an overwritten rejection or manufactured acceptance score.

Remaining approximation: rear surfaces and hidden geometry are inferred from the written
chassis family and shared rig; pearl panels use procedural material factors instead of a
reference texture bake. Browser WebGL lighting and physical mobile performance are separate
checks from these CPU images and the deterministic rig/geometry tests.
